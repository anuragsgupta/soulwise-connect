# Admin Dashboard - Quick Reference

## Dashboard Access by Role

### Super Admin Dashboard
**Access:** Users with `SUPER_ADMIN` role  
**Features:**
- ✅ View all universities
- ✅ Create new universities
- ✅ View institute counts
- ✅ Invite university administrators
- ✅ System-wide statistics
- ✅ Full system access

**Key Actions:**
1. **Create University:** Click "Create University" button
2. **Invite Admin:** Click "Invite Admin" next to any university
3. **View Details:** Click "View Details" to see university information

### University Admin Dashboard
**Access:** Users with `UNIVERSITY_ADMIN` role  
**Features:**
- ✅ Manage institutes under their university
- ✅ View university-wide statistics
- ✅ Invite institute administrators
- ✅ University settings management
- ✅ Reports and analytics

**Key Actions:**
1. **Manage Institutes:** Create, edit, and archive institutes
2. **Institute Admins:** Invite and manage institute administrators
3. **View Reports:** Access university-wide reports

### Institute Admin Dashboard
**Access:** Users with `INSTITUTE_ADMIN` role  
**Features:**
- ✅ Student management (add, edit, bulk upload)
- ✅ Faculty management
- ✅ Course management
- ✅ Bulk operations (CSV upload)
- ✅ Institute-specific reports

**Key Actions:**
1. **Student Management:** Add individual students or bulk upload
2. **Faculty Management:** Invite and manage faculty members
3. **Course Management:** Create and assign courses
4. **Bulk Operations:** Upload student data via CSV

### Faculty Dashboard
**Access:** Users with `FACULTY` role  
**Features:**
- ✅ View assigned courses
- ✅ Student roster access
- ✅ Assignment management
- ✅ Grade management
- ✅ Student interactions

**Key Actions:**
1. **My Courses:** View and manage assigned courses
2. **Student Roster:** Access student information
3. **Assignments:** Create and grade assignments
4. **Grades:** Input and manage student grades

## Quick Tips

### For Super Admins
1. Create universities before inviting other admins
2. Use meaningful domain names (e.g., university.edu)
3. Verify contact information before creating

### For University Admins
1. Create institutes before inviting institute admins
2. Use standard institute codes
3. Keep institute information updated

### For Institute Admins
1. Use bulk upload for large student batches
2. Verify student data before upload
3. Assign faculty to courses appropriately

### For Faculty
1. Keep course information updated
2. Regular grade updates
3. Monitor student engagement
